Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nikita Guzenko ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use our applications that connect to the Pinterest API (the "App"). Website: nikitaguzenko.com.

1. Information we access

When you authorize the App with your Pinterest account, the App may access, only with the permissions you grant:

  • Basic account information (username, account ID).
  • Your boards and Pins (titles, descriptions, links and image URLs).

We do not access your Pinterest password, payment information or private messages.

2. How we use information

The App uses this information solely to retrieve images and Pins you have saved, so you can use them as visual references in your own creative and content-production work. We do not use Pinterest data for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models, and we do not sell, rent or share it with third parties.

3. Storage and retention

Pinterest data is processed on demand. Any cached copies are stored only as long as needed for the requested task and are deleted afterwards. Access tokens are stored securely and can be revoked at any time.

4. Your choices

You can revoke the App's access at any time in your Pinterest account settings (Settings → Security and logins / Apps). You may also contact us to request deletion of any data related to your account.

5. Pinterest

Use of Pinterest data is subject to the Pinterest Developer Guidelines and Pinterest's own Privacy Policy.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
